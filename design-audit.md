# Why this mechanism is separate

AmbiguityBudget is not an intent-deduplication lock: it does not compare retries or reserve execution. It is not a policy-diff tool: neither source is revised. It asks whether one instruction plus one guardrail permits multiple materially different action signatures before execution begins.

Caller-selected URLs are evidence slots, not trusted authorities by default. A production integrator must pin an authentic instruction channel and governing guardrail. Bundled documents are synthetic and explicitly labeled.
